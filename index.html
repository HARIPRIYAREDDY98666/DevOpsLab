Learn / About Quantum

About quantum screening

The concepts behind Q-DRUG's hybrid engine, explained simply

◈

What is quantum computing?

Quantum computers use qubits that can exist in superpositions of 0 and 1, letting them represent and manipulate exponentially large state spaces natively.

⚛

Why molecules are quantum systems

Electrons in a molecule obey quantum mechanics — their correlated behavior is exactly what qubits are suited to represent.

↻

How VQE works

A variational quantum eigensolver alternates between a quantum circuit measuring energy and a classical optimizer adjusting parameters to minimize it.

The VQE loop
Interactive: click a stage
What's a Hamiltonian, really?
An operator encoding every possible energy of a system — its electron kinetic energy, nuclear attraction, and electron repulsion — so that its lowest eigenvalue is the molecule's ground-state energy.
What does Jordan–Wigner mapping do?
It translates fermionic operators (electrons, which anti-commute) into qubit operators (which don't), so the Hamiltonian can run on a quantum circuit.
Why is this only decision support?
Simulated energies approximate real molecular behavior but don't capture solvent effects, binding kinetics, or toxicity — laboratory validation remains essential before any candidate advances.

Frequently asked questions
How accurate is the quantum energy estimate?
On the simulator backend, typical deviation from classical reference calculations is 2–5%, depending on ansatz depth and molecule size.
Can I run a batch of 500 molecules at once?
Yes — upload a CSV on the Screen Molecules page. Batches are queued and processed sequentially on the selected backend.
Does Q-DRUG replace laboratory testing?
No. Q-DRUG is a triage tool that helps you prioritize which candidates deserve laboratory time — it never replaces empirical validation.
